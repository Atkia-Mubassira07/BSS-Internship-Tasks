Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between event design and event planning?

Event design and event planning work hand in hand to deliver a successful event. Event design deals with visual styling and aesthetic aspects, while planning handles logistics, vendor quotes, and execution schedules.

How early should I book the venue?

We recommend booking at least 3 to 6 months in advance for corporate events and 6 to 12 months for weddings.

What is your refund and cancellation policy?

Cancellations made 30 days prior to the event date receive a full deposit refund minus processing fees.

How do you keep budgets on track?

We provide transparent itemized cost sheets and regular updates before committing to any additional expenses.
